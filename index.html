Astable Multivibrator

An astable multivibrator is a free-running oscillator that continuously switches between HIGH and LOW states and generates a square wave output.

Resistance Formula: R = (ADC / 4095) × 5000
Stepper Mapping: steps = map(R, 0, 5000, 0, 2048)
Astable Formula: T = 1.386 RC
Frequency: f = 1 / T
